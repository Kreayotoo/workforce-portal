Amaravati Underground Utility Digital Twin

A reference for engineers, operators and reviewers. It explains what each screen shows, how to move around the three-dimensional model, and — most importantly — how to tell which values are measured and which are simulated.

01Introduction

The Digital Twin is a single browser file that holds the complete underground utility network of the Amaravati capital city — 53 blocks across 11 zones, five utilities, the transmission trunk main, the 190 MLD water treatment plant and its administration building — together with the APCRDA master plan and an operations & maintenance module.

It runs entirely in the browser. Nothing is uploaded; the only internet traffic is for map tiles, fonts, the weather feed and the 3D decoder, and all of those degrade gracefully when unavailable.

Utilities

  • Potable water
  • Sewerage
  • Storm water
  • Reuse water
  • Power / ICT duct bank

Scale

  • 130,000+ pipe segments
  • 44,000+ manholes and chambers
  • 1,106 trunk pipes (2,291 segments), 121 km, up to 2,500 mm · R4 bypass
  • 95,943 master-plan plots, 35 zoning classes

Engineering sources

  • WaterGEMS hydraulic models
  • AutoCAD DXF site plans
  • IFC / Revit building models
  • QGIS shapefiles and style files
Who this manual is forAnyone opening the twin for the first time. Sections 1–4 are enough to start; Section 9 is required reading before quoting any figure from the screen.

02Getting started

Open the file in Chrome or Edge. The application starts on Zone 1A, at 1× vertical exaggeration with the road surface hidden.
Choose what to look at from the left sidebar. Click a zone header for the whole zone, or a single block such as 1A · B10. Special views — Entire City, Trunk + Ring Main, WTP, Admin Building, Weather, Decision Support, O&M — are at the top of the sidebar.
Wait for the loader. A block loads in 1–3 seconds; a zone in 2–8; Entire City in 10–20 seconds on a laptop. The spinner is normal.
Move around. Left-drag pans, the wheel zooms, right-drag tilts. The toolbar on the right edge of the scene gives Top, Isometric and Explode.
Click on any pipe, manhole or structure to read its data in the tooltip and the information panel.
Check the tag on every number. REAL means measured or drawn; SIMULATED means a placeholder value. See Section 9.
Recommended setupChrome or Edge (current), a screen of 1600 px or wider, and a machine with a dedicated or recent integrated GPU. Block views work on tablets; zone and city views are intended for desktops.

063D toolbar

The toolbar runs down the right edge of the scene. Buttons light up when active. Some appear only where they apply.

Top view
Plan view looking straight down. Best for tracing routes and reading the site plan.
Isometric
The default three-quarter view. Re-frames the whole network.
Explode layers
Lifts each utility onto its own shelf so overlapping networks can be inspected separately. Facilities, the WTP and the building are hidden while exploded.
Toggle flow
Animated flow inside the hollow pipe walls, always in the physical direction. Raw water: intake wells → MS 2000 mm main → WTP inlet (sand-coloured). Inside the plant: a schematic arrow path runs inlet → aerator → flocculators → settlers → filters → chlorination → clear water reservoir → pump house. Treated water: R-1 / PMP-1 → trunk main → WDC tapping points, following the WaterGEMS flow arrows REAL; speed from EPANET. Distribution: each block's network flows outward from its feed point, marked FEED ← WDC-xx feed location ASSUMED. Sewer and storm: by gravity from the higher to the lower design invert REAL.
1 : 1 true scale
Draws every pipe at its real diameter instead of a readable minimum. Small-bore pipes become hairlines; useful when comparing sizes.
Above-ground city / Toggle building
Drapes the APCRDA master-plan land-use as a coloured ground layer. In views that contain the admin building, this button instead shows or hides the building.
Site labels
Shows or hides the unit-process and headworks labels over the plant. Appears only in the WTP view.
WTP site
Shows or hides the treatment plant. Appears only in the Trunk, Entire City and WTP views.
Fullscreen 3D
Expands the scene to the whole browser window. Esc returns. The parameter dashboard remains available.
Live EPANET hydraulics
Sends the current network to the server, runs EPANET and colours pipes by result. Requires the server; the model is uncalibrated and indicative only.
OSM buildings
Building massing and roads from OpenStreetMap, for context only. DEMO

07Layers, sliders & panels

Layer chips (top of the network panel)

One chip per utility with its live pipe count. Click to hide or show. Two further chips: Structures (manholes, chambers, sumps — off by default in zone and city views to keep them fast) and Road surface (the formation road level plane).

Sliders

ExaggerationVertical stretch applied to invert levels. Opens at 1× (true). Increase it to see gradients on flat ground. Structures, the WTP and the building stretch with it so the scene stays consistent. Road opacityOpens at 0 %. Raise it to see the road surface over the pipes.

Right-column panels

WTP process controlPlant status, inflow, outflow, running units. Chemical dosingAlum, lime and chlorine dose rates. TurbidityRaw and treated water turbidity. KPIs & trendsLive sparklines for the main indicators, with value axis, time axis and the view's scope on each chart. The window is the last 160 seconds at 2-second samples. Utility throughputFlow per utility for the current view.
Status of panel valuesEvery process value in the right column is SIMULATED until plant SCADA or field sensors are connected through the ingestion service (Section 17).

09Data labelling policy

Honest labelling of measured versus simulated data is a non-negotiable requirement of this platform. Every figure on screen carries one of three tags.

REAL

Taken directly from an engineering source without adjustment: WaterGEMS models, AutoCAD DXF, IFC/Revit BIM, QGIS shapefiles, the APCRDA style file, or connected sensors.

SIMULATED

A plausible, engineering-typical value used because the real one has not been supplied. Suitable for demonstration and layout review — not for operational decisions.

DEMO

Placeholder content present only so the screen shows its intended form: OSM massing, sample work orders, sample readings.

Real today

  • Pipe geometry, diameters, inverts and lengths — all 53 blocks and the trunk main
  • Trunk-main hydraulics from the WaterGEMS R4 result tables — four demand scenarios (2028, 2043, 2043 without Undavalli & Tadepalli, 2058): per-pipe flow, velocity, headloss, material and C; per-junction demand, hydraulic grade and pressure. Switch scenario with the WaterGEMS selector in the twin footer.
  • Master-plan land-use polygons and their APCRDA colour coding
  • WTP site boundary, site-plan linework, unit footprints and positions
  • The twelve trunk nodes inside the WTP site and the 2,500 mm headworks main
  • WTP site model and intake headworks geometry and levels (IFC); admin building geometry, levels and height

Simulated today

  • All process values, dosing, turbidity, pressures and the eight KPI tiles
  • Positions of facilities other than the plant and intake
  • WTP process values (dosing, turbidity, flows)
  • Storm response, leak index, power load
  • Live EPANET results from the server (runs but is not calibrated) — the WaterGEMS tables are the hydraulics of record
Pending from APCRDACommissioning dates, crew names, SLA targets, EPANET Tier-1 data, duct-bank details. Fields that depend on these show a dash or a SIMULATED tag until they are supplied.

11Master plan & GIS map

The GIS MAP tab shows the same network on a Leaflet map with the APCRDA land-use overlay. The overlay is rendered from the official shapefile at 7,200 px, coloured by the ZONING attribute using the APCRDA style file.

  • The legend lists all 35 classes. Hover a row for the plot count.
  • R1 – Village planning zone is drawn with a diagonal hatch, exactly as in the APCRDA style.
  • R5 – Affordable / EWS Housing Zone is marked *: it is absent from the style file, so its colour was assigned and is documented as such.
  • Click a block outline to open that block in 3D. The WTP polygon and marker identify the plant.
  • The admin-building marker can be dragged to test an alternative position; the surveyed position is restored on reload.
Road networkThe APCRDA style specifies the SU2 road network as transparent. The twin draws it in black so the corridors remain visible over the basemap.

12190 MLD water treatment plant

The plant is taken from its AutoCAD site plan and georeferenced to EPSG:32644 against three surveyed corners, with residuals of 0.2 mm easting and 5 mm northing. Site centre 16.497808 N, 80.550870 E; plot envelope 422 × 667 m.

What is shown

  • Site boundary triangle from the survey coordinates
  • Every line and label of the CAD drawing, draped at true scale
  • The plant's full 3D site model from IFC — 19 linked buildings, 13,138 structural and process elements, 1.2 million triangles — with real heights; unit-process labels from the site plan; the admin building at its original detail on its plot
  • The 12 trunk nodes that fall inside the site
  • The raw-water intake: wells 1 & 2 and the headworks from IFC, with the surveyed MS 2000 mm raw-water pipe

Trunk-main tie-in

In the R4 model the source reservoir R-1 lies inside the Clear Water Reservoir footprint and pumping station PMP-1 at the Clear Water Pump House — where a reservoir outlet and pump suction belong. The new bypass main runs from the headworks north-east to the raw-water INTAKE. The CAD drawing and the hydraulic model were produced independently and agree.

Heights are realSince 22 September 2026 the plant is drawn from its IFC site model, so structure heights are real. Model z 0 is the design finished ground level +21.00 m. The intake model is placed by a two-well fit to the surveyed intake wells (residual 0.000 m) and its levels are reduced levels.

13Admin building BIM

The Revit model (IFC4, 118 meshes, 16 levels, 33.38 m) is placed at the ADMIN. BLD. footprint of the site plan — E 452139.91, N 1824212.16, rotated −27.17° to the site grid, base level 23.40 m — at true scale with no stretch. The three coordinates embedded in the delivered IFC files were between 5.9 and 14.3 km from the plant and were not used.

Floors panelIsolate any of the 16 levels, from pile cut-off to terrace. RoomsDisplay spaces from the IFC with their names. Auto-rotate / Reset viewTurntable presentation; return to the default camera. Trunk & City viewsThe building carries a light beacon and a WTP ADMIN BLDG label so it can be found at city scale, where a 30 m building is smaller than one pixel.
If a plain purple block appearsThe building is placed immediately as a massing block of the correct size; the detailed model replaces it when the Draco decoder loads from gstatic.com. A persistent purple block means the decoder was blocked — the position is still correct.

15Decision support

Open Decision Support and tap the map where an incident has occurred — a burst, contamination, power fault or road closure.
A real GIS buffer is drawn and the connected network is traced. Affected pipes, plots and an estimated population (from land-use densities) are listed.
Recommended actions and isolation points appear on the right. The summary can be exported for the shift report.

16Weather & leak detection

Live Weather & Storm Risk retrieves current and forecast rainfall for Amaravati from Open-Meteo REAL and shows how the storm-water network would respond SIMULATED.

DMA Leak Detection estimates non-revenue water from minimum night flow and mass balance on the selected network. It is reached from within Decision Support.

17Server, roles & security

The single file works fully offline. The optional FastAPI server adds live EPANET, sensor ingestion, shared O&M data, automatic backups and user accounts.

RolePermissions
AdminAll functions, including users, backups and security settings
EngineerEdit assets and PM schedules, run hydraulics, verify and close work orders
OperatorCreate and progress work orders, acknowledge alarms, log readings
ViewerRead-only access to every view and report

Readings are stored in readings.db and rolled up automatically — raw kept 90 days, hourly averages 2 years, daily for ever; nothing is deleted before it is rolled up. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256; sessions use HMAC-signed tokens; every change and every export is written to an audit log; backups run every six hours; browser origins are restricted by a CORS allow-list. Details are in server/SECURITY.md and the Login & Security Access document.

19Troubleshooting

SymptomCause and remedy
The spinner never stopsEntire City needs up to 20 s. If a block view hangs, the tab has usually exhausted GPU memory. Close other 3D tabs and reload. Chrome's "Aw, snap" page indicates the renderer was terminated; this is why fly-in and auto-zoom were removed from the city view.
Purple block instead of the admin buildingThe Draco decoder did not download from gstatic.com. Placement is correct; reload when the connection allows it.
Map tiles are blankBasemap tiles require internet. The network, land-use overlay and legend still work offline.
Live EPANET does nothingThe server is not running or the browser origin is not on its allow-list. Start it with server/start.sh and check security.json.
A facility appears mis-placedSimulated facilities snap to the nearest real pipe vertex. Check the underlying block geometry. The WTP and the admin building are surveyed and do not move.
Fonts look differentFonts load from Google Fonts. Offline, the browser substitutes system fonts; nothing else changes.
Nothing happens when a key is pressedClick in the 3D scene first so it has focus, and make sure the cursor is not in a text field.
